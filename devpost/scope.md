# JobWalk Scribe

Talk through a job walk on your phone or laptop; get a client-ready Scope of Work & Proposal letter that only says what you actually said.

## The Unique Kernel
**Traceable scope.** Every line of the client document links back to the exact phrase in the contractor's note it came from, and anything uncertain ("not sure", "maybe", "?") becomes a question for the client instead of a promise. The AI is only allowed to reword; a guard throws out any rewrite that changes, adds or drops a number. Generic AI writers invent scope; this one can't.

## Who It's For
A small residential contractor or carpenter (1–5 person crew) who walks a bathroom or deck with a homeowner, scribbles or mumbles notes in the truck, and then spends 45 minutes that night turning them into a proposal — or skips it and sends a one-line price text, which is where "I thought that was included" disputes start.

## The Core Loop
Walk the job → tap the mic (or type) and say it like you'd say it to your lead carpenter → tap "Write it up" → check the document, click any line to see where it came from → print to PDF or copy into a text/email. Repeat on every estimate.

## Inspiration & Identity
Jobsite-plain: hard-hat yellow on charcoal, a paper-white document in a serif font so it reads like a real letter. No jargon in the UI. Big buttons that work with gloves-off thumbs.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Lee spent years as a carpenter before moving into engineering. The worst part of the trade wasn't the work; it was paperwork at the kitchen table at 10 PM, and the arguments that came from vague scopes.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A messy six-line dictated bathroom note becomes a one-page scope with trade sections, exclusions ("Owner supplies the tile"), an allowance, a hidden-conditions clause triggered by "probably rot", a client question ("Do you want a niche?"), price, duration and signature lines. **The "oh, that's cool" beat:** click "Add GFCI outlet…" in the document and the exact words light up in the original note.

## The POC Boundary
Voice/typed note in; one document type (Scope of Work & Proposal) out; source tracing; optional free AI polish with a number guard; print/copy/download; autosave on device.

## Later
Photos per item, Spanish output, more document types (site-visit report, change-order letter), line-item pricing, PWA install.

## Explicitly Cut
- Paid AI APIs — Lee's zero-overhead rule and privacy of client notes.
- Accounts/cloud sync — not needed to prove the kernel.
- Automatic pricing — would invent numbers, which breaks the kernel.
